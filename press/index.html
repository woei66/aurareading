What it does

Users upload a full-body photo, choose an aura analysis type, and after a short processing wait receive a colorful aura visualization and an entertainment-style interpretation. Results can be saved directly on the device.

Honest positioning: Aura144 is an entertainment app. Visualizations and interpretations are symbolic and creative — they are not medical, diagnostic, or scientifically validated.
