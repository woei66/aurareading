Questions

Before you download.

What kind of photo do I need?

A full-body colour photo of one person, standing and facing the camera with your arms open and palms facing forward. Leave about 30% empty space above your head and below your feet, keep your whole body in frame, and use a plain light-coloured background. Short sleeves and shorts work best. The app includes a shooting guide and checks your photo before you pay — if it does not pass, you are told to upload a better one rather than being charged for a bad reading.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is uploaded to our server, used to produce your aura image and reading, and then deleted. We do not keep your portrait photos and we do not use them to train anything. You can read the details in our privacy policy.

How is this different from a photo filter?

A filter applies a fixed effect to every picture. Aura144 reads the energy field around the body in your photo and renders the colours it finds, then explains them in a written reading. It was developed with Judith Collins, who has worked with the human aura for decades — the interpretation follows that tradition, not a preset.

Who is Judith Collins?

Judith Collins is an Australian aura teacher and one of the best-known authorities on the human aura. She is the aura advisor to Aura144 and worked with us on how the app reads and explains colour. You can read more about her at yourhumanaura.com.

Do I need any special equipment?

No. You need an Android phone and a photo of yourself. No aura camera, no sensors, no studio, nothing to buy. That is the whole point — this is the reading people used to have to travel and pay for.

What does it cost?

Aura144 is free to download. Each reading is unlocked individually inside the app, and the fee is shown on screen before you confirm — so you always know what you are paying for. Nothing is charged automatically.

Why does it sometimes ask me to wait?

To keep the service fair, there is a short cooldown between readings from the same device or network. If you see a message asking you to wait, it usually clears within a couple of minutes.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not at the moment. Aura144 is an Android app, available on Google Play. An iPhone version is not currently planned.
