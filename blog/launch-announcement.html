Aura144: Aura Camera is now available on Google Play

We're happy to announce that Aura144: Aura Camera is now available on Google Play. The free Android app turns a full-body photo into a colorful aura visualization with a playful, entertainment-style interpretation.

How it works

  1. Prepare a full-body color photo (one person, space above head and below feet, plain background).
  2. Choose your preferred aura analysis type.
  3. Upload your photo and enter your question.
  4. In 5–10 minutes, receive your aura image and interpretation.

Results can be saved directly on your device, and the app supports multiple languages for a global audience.

A note on what Aura144 is — and is not

Aura144 is an entertainment app. The aura visualizations and interpretations are symbolic and creative. They are not medical, diagnostic, or scientifically validated. We built it as a fun, colorful way to reflect and explore.

"People enjoy playful ways to reflect on themselves. Aura Camera is a fun, colorful tool for personal exploration." — Aura144 team
