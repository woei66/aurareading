# Aura144 — Aura Camera

Turn a full-body photo into a colorful aura visualization with an entertainment-style interpretation.

- **Platform:** Android
- **Store:** [Google Play — Aura144: Aura Camera](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tripbnb.auracamerapro&utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=aura_6h)
- **Package ID:** `com.tripbnb.auracamerapro`
- **Web:** https://www.aura144.com/

## How it works
1. Prepare a full-body color photo (one person, space above head and below feet, plain background).
2. Choose an aura analysis type.
3. Upload your photo and enter your question.
4. In 5–10 minutes, receive your aura image and interpretation.

Results can be saved directly on your device. The app supports multiple languages.

## What Aura144 is — and is not
Aura144 is an **entertainment** app. Aura visualizations and interpretations are symbolic and creative — they are not medical, diagnostic, or scientifically validated. Enjoy them for fun and self-reflection.
