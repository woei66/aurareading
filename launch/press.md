**Aura144 Launches 'Aura Camera' App That Creates Colorful Aura Visualizations From Photos**

TAIPEI, Taiwan — Aura144 has launched Aura Camera, a new Android app that transforms full-body photos into colorful aura visualizations with entertainment-style interpretations. Designed for fun and self-reflection, the app uses image analysis to build a symbolic aura around the subject and supports more than a dozen languages.

The app guides users through simple photo guidelines, lets them choose an aura analysis type, and returns a personalized aura image and interpretation in 5–10 minutes. Results can be saved directly on the device. Aura Camera is free to download with optional in-app purchases and is rated 3+ on Google Play.

"People enjoy playful ways to reflect on themselves," said a developer at Aura144. "We built Aura Camera as a fun, colorful tool for personal exploration — no claims beyond entertainment."

Aura144 is an entertainment app: aura visualizations and interpretations are symbolic and creative, and are not medical, diagnostic, or scientifically validated.
