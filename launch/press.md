**Aura144 Launches an Aura Camera App Co-Developed with Aura Authority Judith Collins**

TAIPEI, Taiwan — Aura144 has launched Aura144: Aura Camera, an Android app that reads the energy field around the
body from a single full-body photograph and renders it as an aura image, together with a written interpretation
covering the emotional, mental and spiritual layers. The app was developed with Judith Collins, the Australian aura
teacher and one of the best-known authorities on the human aura, who serves as aura advisor to Aura144.

Unlike a photo filter, which applies a fixed effect to any image, Aura144 requires a photograph taken to a specific
framing — the subject standing, arms open, palms facing forward, against a plain light-coloured background — and
validates that photograph before any payment is taken. The app then produces the aura image and a written reading.
Results are saved on the user's device, can be shared, and can be annotated with private notes.

"There are two ways to build something in this category," said LIN WEI TING, the developer of Aura144. "You can
dress up an effect and hope nobody asks questions, or you can be specific about what you are doing and who guided
it. We worked with Judith Collins because the reading itself — how colour is interpreted and how it is explained —
is the product. The image is just how you see it."

The app offers ten readings across four areas: personal readings, relationship readings using two photographs,
family readings using up to five photographs, and a single-photograph daily aura. Aura144 is free to download, with
each reading unlocked individually inside the app and the fee displayed on screen before confirmation. There is no
subscription and no automatic charge.

Since its launch in March 2026, Aura144 has been installed by more than 28,000 people, with daily installations
growing from a handful per day to approximately 240. Users are spread across more than 190 countries and regions,
and the app is available in 15 languages including English, Traditional and Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Russian,
Spanish, Arabic and Persian.

Aura144 states plainly that its readings are an energy-based interpretation offered for self-reflection and
personal insight, and are not a medical, psychological, scientific or professional diagnosis. Uploaded photographs
are processed to generate the requested result and then deleted from the server; the app does not use facial
recognition or build any database of face data.
