**Tagline:** Turn your full-body photo into a colorful aura visualization.

Aura144 is a free Android app that transforms full-body photos into unique aura visualizations with entertainment-style interpretations. Upload a full-body photo, choose an aura analysis type, and after a short processing wait receive a colorful aura image you can save to your device. Multilingual (12+ languages), free with optional in-app purchases, rated 3+.

How it works:
1. Prepare a full-body color photo (space above head and below feet, plain background).
2. Choose an aura analysis type.
3. Upload and get your aura visualization in 5–10 minutes.

Honest positioning: Aura144 is an entertainment app — visualizations and interpretations are symbolic and creative, not medical or scientific.
