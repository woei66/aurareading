written reading across the emotional, mental and spiritual layers. It was developed with Judith Collins, the
Australian aura teacher and one of the best-known authorities on the human aura.

How it works:
1. Take a full-body photo the way the in-app guide shows you — standing, arms open, palms forward, plain light background.
2. Choose one of ten readings: personal, relationship (two photos), family (up to five photos), or a daily aura.
3. The app validates your photo before payment, then returns your aura image and written reading in a few minutes.

Free to download, 15 languages, results saved on your device, each reading unlocked individually with the price
shown before you pay — no subscription, no automatic charge.

Honest positioning: aura imagery and readings are an energy-based interpretation offered for self-reflection, not
a medical, psychological or scientific diagnosis.
